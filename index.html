Interpretation & limitations

This is a preliminary planning tool, not a certified architectural or clinical capacity standard. The simulation uses 3,000 simulated days per scenario and fixed random seed 42, with hourly Poisson arrivals and independent lognormal occupancy durations. P95 is computed from simulated hourly concurrent occupancy over the observation period, not from average occupancy. Validate with real timestamped arrivals, bed-entry/exit times, boarding data, staffing, and local design requirements before budgeting.

Baseline horizontal beds = ceiling(projected daily visits × mean occupancy hours / (24 × target utilization)). Design horizontal beds = max(baseline beds, simulated occupancy percentile in surge scenario). Green stations = ceiling(peak hourly arrivals during operating hours / (service rate × utilization)); this is a peak-throughput proxy, not a queueing simulation. Surge capacity may be flex capacity rather than fully staffed permanent beds. No MCI-specific scenario, patient transfers between zones, admission bed blocking dynamics, seasonal demand, or staff scheduling is simulated. For Green, arrivals outside operating hours require a separate routing plan.
